# Contributing

Useful contributions include reproducible orchestration failures, safer ownership or recovery rules, current host-contract corrections, clearer customer paths, portability fixes, and stronger discriminating evals.

Before opening a change, describe the behavior and failure it prevents, identify the affected package surface, include synthetic evidence when possible, keep unrelated cleanup out of the change, run the narrowest relevant checks, and remove credentials or private data.

By submitting a contribution, you represent that you have authority to submit it and agree that an accepted contribution may be distributed under the license applicable to the part of the repository it modifies, unless Collaborative Dynamics agrees otherwise in writing.
