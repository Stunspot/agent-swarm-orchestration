# Security policy

Do not open a public issue containing credentials, private keys, customer records, proprietary source you are not authorized to share, exploit payloads against third parties, or other sensitive material.

For ordinary defects, provide the smallest synthetic reproduction through the [issue tracker](https://github.com/Stunspot/agent-swarm-orchestration/issues). For a suspected vulnerability that cannot be safely disclosed in public, request a private reporting route through [Collaborative Dynamics](https://collaborative-dynamics.com).

The Augment does not grant authority to test a system. Active security work requires an authorized target, environment, time window, rate and concurrency limits, prohibited actions, data-handling rules, and stop contact.
